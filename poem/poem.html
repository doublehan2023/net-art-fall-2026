Love Yourself for Who You Are

A poem of self-acceptance

You were born to be who you are.
Embrace it.

You are a beautiful human.
Your unique beauty is yours alone.

You have many gifts and talents to share.
Share them in your own special way.

Your story is yours alone.
Use it to help others with theirs.

Surround yourself with people who truly see your best self.
Shine your bright light on the world for all to see.

You are a miracle.
Love yourself for who you are!

Small reminders

  • Your voice matters.
  • Your kindness has value.
  • Your growth is worth celebrating.

A practice for today

  1. Take a deep breath.
  2. Name one thing you appreciate about yourself.
  3. Carry that thought with you.

Written by Laurie Jonas
