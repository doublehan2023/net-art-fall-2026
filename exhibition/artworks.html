Douglas Davis · 1994

The World's First Collaborative Sentence

The World's First Collaborative Sentence is an early Net Art project that invited internet users to contribute to an ongoing collective sentence. Instead of being created entirely by one artist, the artwork developed through contributions from people online. This project demonstrates how the internet can transform the audience from passive viewers into active participants.

Aaron Koblin · 2006

The Sheep Market

The Sheep Market is an online art project that collected 10,000 drawings of sheep from people using Amazon Mechanical Turk. Each participant was paid a small amount to draw a sheep, and the drawings were brought together into one large digital collection. The project explores online collaboration while also raising questions about creativity, labor, and the value of individual artistic contributions.

Aaron Koblin and Takashi Kawashima · 2008

Ten Thousand Cents

Ten Thousand Cents is a digital artwork created by Aaron Koblin and Takashi Kawashima in 2008. The artists invited 10,000 anonymous online workers to recreate small sections of a $100 bill without knowing what the final image would be. Each participant was paid one cent, and their individual drawings were combined to form one complete artwork. The project explores how small contributions can form a larger collective artwork while raising questions about online labor, collaboration, and the value of creativity.
