HER CLASSMATE


We weren't friends.

I don't think we ever spoke outside of school.


But I remember seeing her that summer.

August 14.


She was standing across the street from the gas station.

Her clothes were completely wet.


I almost went over to ask if she was okay.

But when I looked again,
she was gone.



Her sister says she left four days earlier.



Maybe I have the date wrong.
